that the package has externally validated every future use case.

## How These References Are Used Here

- RFC 6962 supports the declared Merkle tree convention used by `fcg/fcg.py`:
  leaf hash `sha256(0x00 || record)`, internal node hash
  `sha256(0x01 || left || right)`, and auditable inclusion paths.
- MMR is cited as the established reranking idea behind future diversity-aware
  evidence selection. The hackday ranking itself is still the direct
  restoration-distance result unless a second-stage score is explicitly shown.
- The Zenodo FCO/FCG records are cited as operator-supplied design lineage for
  custody objects, graph integration, AI-output custody classification, and
  bounded evidence packaging.

## Claim Boundary

These citations support provenance, custody, reranking design, and evidence
packaging. They do not support therapeutic efficacy, biological rescue,
clinical utility, legal FTO opinions, or claims that live Convoke/Open Targets
joins are already wired unless the demo shows those records.
